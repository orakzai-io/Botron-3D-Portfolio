HUMAN, BEHIND THE MACHINE

Long before writing code, my life was measured in hundredths of a second. Spending ten years on starting blocks as a National Swimmer and Gold Medalist taught me that discipline is a daily standard, not a switch you flip. Today, as a Full-Stack AI Engineer and Computer Science student (4.0 CGPA), I bring that same obsession with precision to software. I don't just consume AI APIs. I build the systems that power them, from high-performance RAG pipelines on pgvector to async ingestion handling 50k+ daily records in production.

Beyond engineering, I believe the best builders understand people. As IT Manager and Head Coach for the KP Swimming Association, mentoring 30+ competitive swimmers has taught me real-time leadership, patience, and clear communication under pressure. When I step away from the terminal, you will usually find me across a chessboard, training the same tactical foresight and pattern recognition I rely on to architect software.
